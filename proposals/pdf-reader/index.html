Penny reads a PDF statement

A test of SpenDroid's PDF importer. Drop a bank or card statement here and see what Penny makes of it: the transactions, and whether the statement's own totals agree.

Drop PDF statements here, or

Your statement is read inside this browser and never uploaded anywhere. The page only downloads pdf.js, Mozilla's PDF reader, to do the reading. "Copy anonymised layout" swaps every letter for x and every digit for 9, keeping only words like Date, Amount and CR, so you can share the shape of a statement Penny gets wrong without sharing what's on it.
